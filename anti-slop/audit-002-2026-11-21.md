# Audit 002, 2026-11-21, Colour token and adoption audit (AFTER mode)

Scope: the askperi-system adoption (`docs/askperi-adoption.md`) and every colour token in
`src/app/globals.css` and their use across `src/app/*` and `src/components/*`.
Admin screens excluded, as in audit 001. Continues where audit 001 stopped, which left the
palette out of scope on purpose ("Palette is explicitly out of scope here; it gets its own
audit later").

Mode: AFTER. Nothing was changed. Only the numbers the owner approves get fixed.
Priority rule: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.
Numbers are per file: audit 002 has its own H1.., M1.., L1.. sequence.

## Method (evidence, not eyeballing)

- Contrast: `antislop-human/contrast-check.py` for flat pairs, and the WCAG formula applied to
  alpha-composited pairs (a translucent colour on its real background).
- Rendered scan at 1440x1000 and 390x844 over the whole homepage and `/services/pet-transport`:
  every text node's computed colour composited over its effective background, every element
  with a `box-shadow`, every border-radius value, every link target, every `<img>`.
  Raw output in `/tmp/peri/probe/audit-home-1440.json`, `audit-home-390.json`, `audit-tr.json`.
- Logo: pixel histogram of `public/images/logo-pawcab.png` at native 957x797
  (69,175 opaque pixels).

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

### H1: The logo is the least legible thing on the page (R-25, R-20)
The amber mark sits inside a 40px white circle: `#FEBB00` on `#FFFFFF` is **1.70:1**, against the
3:1 minimum for meaningful non-text graphics. On the lime header field directly it would be
**1.01:1**. The identity asset reads as a pale smudge while the type around it is at 8:1.

### H2: The accent token is not the logo colour (R-29, R-31, R-20)
`--color-pawcab-gold` resolves to `#b2d235` (lime, hue ~76) while the mark is `#FEBB00` (amber,
hue ~45). The two are 1.01:1 apart in luminance, so they read as one muddy yellow in two hues:
no hierarchy between accent and mark, and two competing yellows in the same viewport. The palette
is currently the reference site's lime, not this product's amber.

### H3: "Regulars in the back seat." is invisible (R-25)
`GallerySection.tsx:52` renders the `h2` as `#1C3F1D` on `#1C3F1D` (**1:1**). The base layer sets
the heading colour and this heading carries no light-surface override, so it disappears into the
forest band. Measured at 1440 and 390. This predates the adoption (the old base layer had the same
colour rule), but it is live now.

### H4: Em dash in rendered UI text (R-02)
`BaliZoneMap.tsx:149,155` render `", Zone 2, Canggu, Kerobokan"` and `", ...areas"`. The character
ships to users twice on the homepage.

### H5: 16 of 52 tap targets are under 44px at 390px (R-03)
Registered elements below the minimum in one dimension: the address inputs (42px tall), the logo
link (41px), and small inline links (12-16px, including the OpenStreetMap credit). Inputs and the
logo link are the ones worth fixing; the credit link is an attribution edge case.

### H6: Three text pairings fail AA (R-25)
- footer legal line, white/45 on forest: **3.70:1** (needs 4.5 at 12.5px)
- header kicker "Private pet rides, Bali", forest/70 on lime: **3.56:1** (needs 4.5 at 11px)
- header hours "Daily 07.00 to 21.00", forest/75 on lime: **3.95:1** (needs 4.5 at 13px)

### H7: Token names no longer mean what they say (R-29, R-31)
`--color-pawcab-gold` is lime, `--color-brass` is a mid green (`#2a4726`), and the alias block has
no comment saying which value answers to the logo. The next person to reach for "gold" gets lime
again. This is the mechanism that produced H2.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

### M1: No direction file and no declared dials (R-37, R-31)
There is no `DESIGN.md`. `docs/askperi-adoption.md` records reasons for the tokens, the shadow and
the motion, which is the material R-31 asks for, but it declares no
`Dial: ENERGY / RHYTHM / MOTION` line, so R-05 and R-19 cannot be checked against a claim.

### M2: Eight radii on one page, so radius is decoration (R-11)
Rendered at 1440: 9999px pill x29, 28px x24, 12px x10, 16px x3, 8px x2, 75px x2, 32px x1, 18px x1.
Source adds more: 173 `rounded-xl`, 57 `rounded-2xl`, 23 `rounded-lg`. Radius was meant to be the
hierarchy tool in this system and it is currently a grab bag.

### M3: The accent is everywhere (R-29 excessive accent)
On the homepage alone: 21 `bg-pawcab-gold` plus 7 `text-pawcab-gold` elements, and lime is the
header field, the CTA fill, the marker dot, the rule, the underlines, the icon colour and the gallery
numbering. An accent that appears in seven roles is the base colour, not an accent.

### M4: Three icon libraries in one product (R-04)
`react-icons/bs` (25 imports), `react-icons/pi` (11), `react-icons/io5` (2). Three stroke weights and
three optical sizes on the same page, chosen by import convenience.

### M5: Two typographic voices for labels (R-06)
The homepage moved to sentence-case rule labels with a lime dash, while pricing and the service pages
keep `uppercase tracking-widest` / `tracking-[0.2em]` micro-labels (11 in `PetTransportPricing`, 7 in
the transport page). R-06 lists uppercase wide tracking as a default; having both voices is worse than
either one.

### M6: Elevation ranks are undocumented (R-12)
20 elements carry a shadow, at three offset ranks (4px, 5px, 10px) plus the 20px video. The motif is
intentional and repeated, which is what makes it an identity, but nothing records what each rank means,
so a fourth rank will appear the next time someone needs emphasis.

### M7: Text over the transport photo band is unverified (R-25)
The highlights strip sits on a photo under a gradient that is only opaque between 28% and 72% of its
height. The four tiles sit near that boundary, so their real worst-spot contrast cannot be computed
from tokens: it needs a screenshot check or a solid plate behind the text.

### M8: Light-only theme with no recorded reason (R-21)
No `prefers-color-scheme` handling and no toggle. R-21 allows a fixed theme when the product justifies
it; this one is a guest-facing travel service used outdoors in daylight, which is a defensible reason,
but it is not written down anywhere.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

### L1: Three buttons carry a decorative arrow (R-08)
The arrow appears on "See pet transport", "See dog walking", "See pet sitting" and in the pricing CTA.
One consistent place for it would be an identity; three places make it ornament.

### L2: One image ships unsized (R-40)
The Unsplash passenger image in the gallery has no `width`/`height` and its wrapper reports
`aspect-ratio: auto`, so late layout is possible. The other 25 images are sized or aspect-boxed.

### L3: Process note on R-33
The palette migration was applied with a one-off `python3` string replacement over `src`, not by hand.
Nothing ships that patches CSS at runtime and the end state is ordinary source, but the process is the
pattern R-33 warns about and it is why H3 and H7 exist: a blind replace cannot see that one heading on a
dark band needed the opposite colour.

### L4: Type alias names drift (R-31)
`--font-fraunces` and `--font-playfair` now resolve to Cormorant Garamond, and `.font-elmessiri` too.
They work, and the aliases are deliberate for compatibility, but the names describe faces that are no
longer loaded.

### L5: No recorded click-through for this adoption (R-35)
`npm run build` passes and pages were screenshotted, but there is no element-by-element list of what
happened when each control was exercised after the restyle. R-35 requires that list with the deliverable.

## PASS (evidence, so the fix list stays honest)

- R-24 / R-26: 0 dead links, 0 `href="#"`, 0 hash links without a matching element (checked across all 52 interactive elements).
- R-25 structure: exactly 1 `h1`, no skipped heading levels on `/`.
- R-03: no horizontal overflow at 1440 or 390 (`scrollWidth === innerWidth` at both).
- R-27: the address search has idle / searching / ready / empty / error states; the calculator has an empty state.

## Colour proposal (not applied; waits for approval)

Aligned to the measured mark `#FEBB00`:

```
--color-accent:      #FEBB00   logo amber, single accent
--color-accent-ink:  #1C3F1D   forest on amber, 6.93:1
--color-forest:      #1C3F1D   unchanged, keeps 8:1 on sage and 6.93:1 against amber
--color-sage:        #CEE2C5   unchanged
```

Rules the measurements impose:
- amber never carries white text (1.70:1) and is never text on white or sage (1.70 / 1.24:1)
- amber always carries forest ink (6.93:1), and amber text only ever sits on forest (6.93:1)
- the logo therefore needs either a forest chip on a light header, or a forest header
- lime `#B2D235` has no remaining job once the accent is amber: it must be dropped or demoted to a
  chart tint with a written reason, otherwise the page keeps two yellows (H2)
- the `pawcab-gold` token must either be renamed `accent` or be set to `#FEBB00` and documented as
  the logo colour (H7)
